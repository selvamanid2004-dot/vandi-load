How It Works

Moving your load with Vandi Load is quick and simple. Here is how we take care of everything:

1

Tell Us About Your Load

Tell us what you want to send (e.g. 50 boxes, bundles, furniture) and your pickup and delivery locations.

2

We Find the Right Vehicle

Our team reviews your load details and selects the perfect vehicle size so you do not overpay or run out of space.

3

We Arrange Your Driver

We assign a verified, reliable driver with a clean, road-ready vehicle for your trip.

4

Your Load Gets Picked Up

The vehicle reaches your doorstep or warehouse on time and your goods are safely loaded.

5

Your Load Reaches Its Destination

Your goods arrive safely at the destination on time with zero hassle.
